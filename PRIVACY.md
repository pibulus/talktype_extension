# TalkType Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 15, 2025

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TalkType ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our voice-to-text Chrome extension ("TalkType" or "the Extension"). We are committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected.

## Information We Collect

TalkType is designed with privacy as a priority. We collect the following information:

1. **Audio Data**: When you explicitly activate the microphone by clicking the record button, we temporarily collect audio data for the purpose of transcription. This audio data is:
   - Only collected when you actively choose to record
   - Only used for generating text transcriptions
   - Never stored permanently
   - Processed via your personal Gemini API key

2. **API Key**: We store your Gemini API key locally in your browser's secure storage. This key:
   - Remains on your device
   - Is never transmitted to our servers
   - Is only used to authenticate with the Gemini API for transcription requests

3. **Extension Settings**: We store your preference settings (e.g., theme choice, enabled sites) locally on your device.

## How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

1. To provide the voice-to-text transcription functionality
2. To authenticate your requests with the Gemini API
3. To save your preferences for a better user experience

## Information Sharing

TalkType does not share your data with third parties except as follows:

1. **Gemini API**: Audio data is sent to the Gemini API (operated by Google) for transcription purposes only. This occurs only when you explicitly initiate recording. Please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for information on how they handle data.

## Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

1. Your API key is stored securely in your browser's local storage
2. Audio data is transmitted using secure HTTPS connections
3. No audio data or transcriptions are permanently stored by TalkType

## Your Rights

As a user of TalkType, you have the right to:

1. Know what data we collect (as outlined in this policy)
2. Access the source code of the extension (as an open-source project)
3. Uninstall the extension at any time, which will remove all locally stored preferences

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
