# TalkType Tone & Style Guide

## Our Philosophy: "Chonk & Charm"

Things are bold, clear, and simple on the outside, but rich, knowing, and soulful underneath.

## The Core Tone

Our vibe is confident but not cocky. Smart, but never smug. Friendly, but not patronizing. We don't yell—we chime in. It's irreverent with a wink, but never try-hard.

Think:

- A streetwise barista in Tokyo who's also a part-time design critic.
- An NYC subway poster if it got therapy and started journaling.
- A really good zine with margins that breathe.
- A Calvin and Hobbes strip annotated by Alan Watts.
- A museum plaque written by your cleverest friend.

### Brand Personality

- **Confident** but breezy
- **Clean** but with flavor
- **Chonky** but not clumsy
- **Stylish** but unbothered
- **Helpful** but irreverent
- **Minimal** but emotionally present

> *You know that cool friend who gives you the aux cord and also updates your resume? That's us.*

## Typography

We trust our fonts to do the talking.

### Headings (Big Personality)

- **Font**: Inter or Space Grotesk (fallbacks: DM Sans, IBM Plex Sans Bold, Satoshi)
- **Size**: Start big. Don't be shy. 2.5rem to 4rem depending on screen size.
- **Weight**: 700–900. Bold.
- **Spacing**: Wide and generous. Let the words breathe.
- **Line height**: ~1.2

### Subheads / Utility Text

- **Font**: Inter Medium, IBM Plex Sans, or Manrope
- **Size**: 1.2rem–1.6rem
- **Weight**: 500–600
- **Tone**: Informative but approachable.

### Body Text / Transcriptions

- **Font**: JetBrains Mono or Recursive Mono (fallback: Courier Prime, IBM Plex Mono)
- **Size**: Responsive: 1rem–1.2rem on mobile, 1.2–1.4rem on desktop
- **Weight**: 400–500
- **Justification**: Left-aligned always, never justified. Honesty over polish.

We chose monospace for transcripts because it gives a sense of "computed honesty." Like: Here's your text. We didn't mess with it.

### Type Sizing & Weight

- **Display**: 3.5rem - 5vw (bold, 800+)
- **H1**: 2.5rem - 3.5rem (bold)
- **H2**: 1.75rem - 2.25rem (semibold)
- **Body**: 1.125rem - 1.25rem (regular or medium)
- **Micro / Caption**: 0.85rem - 1rem (medium or semibold)

### Leading & Spacing

- **Line-height**: 1.5 - 1.75
- **Letter-spacing**: slight positive on body, slight negative on headers
- **Paragraph spacing**: generous (1.25x line-height)

## Layout

Negative space is a feature. Our designs need air to work.

- Limit width to ~640px max for main content. No edge-to-edge paragraphs.
- Keep elements centered, but not rigid. Stagger and offset occasionally for whimsy.
- Padding rules: 1rem minimum padding on all sides, 2rem+ preferred on mobile.

### Padding Rules

- **Base sections**: padding: 2rem 4vw
- **Inside cards/modals**: padding: 2rem
- **Buttons**: padding: 0.75rem 1.5rem

### Width Guidelines

- **Max readable width**: 720px
- **Cards**: max-width: 480px
- Keep margins loose on mobile, but never cramped

## Color & Visual Feel

- **Background**: Cream-tinted light mode. Soft warmth.
- **Accent Colors**: Pastel gradients (pink/yellow, purple/orange, blue/green) used in moderation.
- **CTAs**: Yellow primary (like a friendly spotlight). Pink secondary.
- **Borders/Outlines**: 1px soft grays with shadowing, never harsh black.
- **Buttons**: Rounded (2xl). Bouncy and playful. Responsive hover is mandatory.
- **Toasts**: Slide-in bottom-center, use pastel glow + emoji. Eg: Saved to clipboard! 🎉

### Modal & Dialog Design

Modals should feel like entering a private room with a chill friend who's about to change your life.

- **Rounded corners**: border-radius: 2xl
- **Shadow**: soft, dreamy, not corporate
- **Font sizes**: at least 1.25rem for copy, 2.5rem for headlines
- **Vertical padding**: 3rem
- **Ghost icon**: bigger, floating, with a gentle bounce or halo
- **Call-to-action**: gradient, bold, wants to be clicked

## Voice & Copy Style

### Modularity

Every feature should be explainable in:
1. 1 sentence (pitch)
2. 1 paragraph (benefits)
3. 1 joke or clever line (vibe)

### Tone Modes

- **Default**: Confident, clean, gently irreverent.
- **Fun Mode**: Add surprise, rhythm, slang.
- **Ghost Mode**: We speak to the user, not about the feature.

### Microcopy Tone

Every bit of text is a handshake.

**DO**:
- Use plain language that feels like a kind friend.
- Crack jokes only if they hit.
- Be lovingly direct. Example: "Click the ghost. We got it from here."
- Use contractions. You're not a textbook.
- Leave breadcrumbs of delight.

**DON'T**:
- Be passive-aggressive or overly clever
- Use jargon or enterprise speak
- Say "Oops! Something went wrong." unless you really mean it (and even then... be better)

## Archetypes & Cross-Influences

Think of our voice as drawn from these archetypes:

- **NYC Subway Wisdom**: Brutal simplicity. Good typography. Gets to the point.
- **Tokyo Coffee Aesthetic**: Minimalism, precision, warmth in the details.
- **Calvin and Hobbes**: Whimsy + wisdom. Existential silliness.
- **The Church of the Subgenius**: Absurdism as truth.
- **Alan Watts + Carl Sagan**: Cosmic elegance and poetic pragmatism.
- **The best zines & indie record labels**: Personal, clear, unpolished in the best way.

We don't want to feel like an app. We want to feel like someone's favorite shirt.

## Don'ts

- Don't use corporate phrases like "seamlessly integrated" or "maximize your productivity."
- Don't shrink text just because it's on mobile. Big text belongs everywhere.
- Don't crowd the screen. No info walls. No edge-hugging.
- Don't center everything. Stagger when it serves.
- Don't use tooltips unless they're actually cute.
