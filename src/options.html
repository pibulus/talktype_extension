How to Use TalkType

Context Menu Method

  1. Add your API key above and hit Save
  2. Test your mic by clicking Test Microphone
  3. Go to any website with text fields
  4. Right-click in a text field and select "Transcribe with TalkType"
  5. Start speaking - the indicator shows it's recording
  6. Click anywhere to stop and insert your text

Popup Method

  1. Click the TalkType icon in your browser toolbar
  2. Hit the "Record & Transcribe" button
  3. Speak while the visualizer shows your voice levels
  4. Click "Stop Recording" when finished
  5. Your text appears and gets copied to clipboard
  6. Paste anywhere you need it
